Hi, I'm Shreshta.

“The very same mush of cells in my brain writing these words is the reason for the unending pangs of lunacy that I perform.”

I study neuroscience and engineering at Brown, and I want to build brain–computer interfaces that give people back what they've lost.

I'm drawn to the place where biology meets machines, and to the questions that come with it: what a thought is, and who should be allowed to read one.

Some things I'm working on:

  • Decoding attention from EEG signals.
  • Research on reading movement intent from the motor cortex.
  • An essay on neural privacy.

Outside the lab I read books about the mind and watch films about it too.
